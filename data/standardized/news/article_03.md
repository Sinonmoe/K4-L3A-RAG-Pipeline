## 1. IELTS Writing Task 2 Test Information

Learn about your IELTS writing task 2 test. All lessons and tips on this page are for both Academic and GT writing task 2.

1. IELTS Writing has two tasks: Task 1 (a report) and Task 2 (an essay).
2. The total time is one hour for both tasks. You should spend only 40 mins on task 2.
3. You manage the time for yourself in IELTS writing. No one will tell you when to move from task 1 to task 2.
4. The essay is a formal style essay. You should not use informal language in your essay.
5. The instructions say “write at least 250 words”. This means you need to write over 250 words.
6. You will be given a topic and instructions. See the sample task below.
7. Writing task 2 is worth about 66% of your total writing score. Click here: [Total Writing Score Calculations](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-scoring/)
8. There are four marking criteria: [WRITING T2 BAND SCORES & MARKING WITH TIPS.](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-task-2-band-scores-5-to-8/) Make sure you learn all about how the examiner will mark your essay.
